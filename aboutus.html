About Talent Search

Talent Search connects people with short-term opportunities across initiatives, workshops, events, and collaborations.

What is Talent Search?

Talent Search is an internal platform designed to discover, post, and participate in short-term opportunities such as initiatives, workshops, events, and collaborations based entirely on skills and interests.

Why we built this

We built this to address fragmented opportunities, underutilized skills, and limited cross-team collaboration. The platform lowers barriers to participation and encourages organic collaboration across teams and disciplines.

What you can do on the platform

  • Discover initiatives, workshops, and events tailored to your profile
  • Contribute efficiently based on your unique skills and availability
  • Post new opportunities and effortlessly find perfect collaborators
  • Earn experience points (XP) and peer recognition through active participation

Our Vision

We aim to build a culture of involvement, shared ownership, and continuous learning — where everyone can contribute, discover hidden talents, and grow through short-term collaborative opportunities.
